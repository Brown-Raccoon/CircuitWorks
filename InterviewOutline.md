# **Interview Outline and Guide to Successful Interviews**

Make your to introduce yourselves in a friendly matter not just because it is courteous but a genuine conversation will flow much better.
Stay on topic: CircuitWorks is a game, talk about experiences with other games or games in a similar genre. (Industry machinery and learning stuff)

# **Possible Structure for an Interview**
Being with background experience with video games in a general sense
Learn what makes a game enjoyable to them
Learn why a specific genre interests them the most, if they show this interest
Ask for any problems they've had with a game, any aspect of the game works from graphics, gameplay mechanics to sound effects
Learn specific features that make a game stand out to them 

--This is a general structure that does not need to be followed

# **Specific Interview Questions**
## How long have you been playing video games for?
## How much time do you spend per week playing video games?
## Do you tend to stick to a genre of games?
## In this specific game, what do you dislike in regards to the progression?
## Does platform availability affect your purchasing or playing decision?
## Do you enjoy any type of game?
## Do you expect to learn something of value from playing a game?
## Do you enjoy playing the same game with other people?
## What is the best moment you can think of when playing with other people?
--Remember these questions do not need to be strictly followed or asked
--Remember to ask open ended questions and follow ups; for example, the second question can be followed with What day do you spend the most time playing?

# **1. Introduction**
This teams software will address the lack of games that provide real world learning while still being an enjoyable experience. Games are becoming more and more of a popular hobby. More people playing games means more developers will make games. Different genres of video games provide different kinds of entertainment within said game, one aspect of a game that sees little use is providing the player with content that has real world experience. This project will take the shape of a game and allow players to learn basic mechanics of industrial machinery with some added programming. Players will walkaway with more experience in industrial machines and general programming. 

# **2. Initial Value Proposition and Customer Segments
## Value Proposition Initial Guess
CircuitWorks is designed to be an enjoyable game all while letting users learn at their own pace

## Customer Segments
People who play video games that appreciate both learning and enjoy spending time playing
